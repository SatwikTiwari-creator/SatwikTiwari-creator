### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a B.Tech CSE student specializing in Cybersecurity at Chandigarh University, obsessed with building secure, scalable applications. Whether I'm configuring Kali Linux VMs for network traffic analysis or spinning up Docker containers for React/Node.js apps, I focus on the intersection of robust engineering and defensive security. 

*   🛡️ **Current Focus:** Blue team defense, penetration testing (Metasploit, Burp Suite), and advanced log analysis.
*   💻 **Building:** Currently scaling **Kisan Hit** (AI-powered smart farming) and refining **Silent Sentinel** (AI women's safety app). 
*   🎓 **Milestones:** Smart India Hackathon Finale Qualifier & Google Cloud Certified.
*   🎥 **Beyond Code:** When I'm not tracking packets in Wireshark, you'll find me managing event cinematography, writing scripts, or blasting Seedhe Maut and Karan Aujla.
